
🎬 YOUR FIRST VIDEO – STARTUP PERSPECTIVE SHIFT
🔥 Hook (0–3s | don’t blink moment)

“Quick question—
who told you a startup needs money first?”

(pause, slight smile)

🎥 Main Script

“Because every startup I’ve ever seen actually starts like this…”

“You’re frustrated.
Something feels dumb.
Something wastes your time.”

“And instead of complaining, you think—
‘Why does nobody fix this?’”

“That thought?
That’s the startup.”

😄 Light Humor (very important for first impression)

“Not the logo.
Not the pitch deck.
Not the MacBook.
Not the ‘Day 1’ post.”

“If those came first,
we’d all be billionaires by now.”

🧠 Perspective Flip (core identity moment)

“A startup isn’t about chasing money.
It’s about removing friction from someone’s life.”

“Money is just the side-effect of being useful…
at scale.”

🎯 Strong Close (brand-setting line)

“So if you’re waiting to be ‘ready’—
don’t.”

“Startups don’t begin with confidence.
They begin with curiosity.”

🚀 Final Line (INTRODUCTION without saying ‘hi I’m…’)


“This page isn’t here to flood you with information.”

“It’s here to make you aware—
of every small thing,
every decision,
every atom
you’re going to hit in world of startups and business.”
